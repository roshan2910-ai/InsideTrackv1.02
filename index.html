Our Mission

Preserving the Stories That Matter.

Founded by Meetu Jain, Inside Track is envisioned as a digital museum dedicated to preserving the history, evolution and impact of journalism from 1993 to 2026. Through historical archives, journalist profiles, multimedia exhibitions and research collections, Inside Track aims to make journalism's legacy accessible to future generations.
